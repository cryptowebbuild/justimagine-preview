Privacy policy.

How Just Imagine Heating Ltd collects, uses and protects your personal information.

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Who we are

Just Imagine Heating Ltd (“we”, “us”) is a heating and plumbing company based in Rugby, Warwickshire, and is the data controller for the information you share with us. You can contact us at justimagineheating@gmail.com or 07774 079 152.

What we collect

  • Your name, phone number, email address and postcode when you contact us or request a quote.
  • Details about your property and the work you need, including notes and photos you choose to send.
  • Records of work carried out, quotes, invoices and gas safety certificates.
  • Basic technical information such as pages visited, if analytics is enabled with your consent.

Why we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and provide a quote.
  • To carry out and record the work, including legal gas safety documentation.
  • To contact you about appointments, follow-up work and reminders you have asked for.
  • To meet our legal and regulatory obligations, including Gas Safe requirements.

Our lawful basis

We rely on contract where the processing is necessary to provide the service you have asked for, legitimate interests for responding to enquiries and running our business, consent for optional analytics and marketing, and legal obligation for records we are required to keep.

How long we keep it

Enquiry details are kept for up to 24 months. Job records, invoices and gas safety documentation are kept for as long as required for tax, warranty and regulatory purposes. You can ask us to delete information we are not legally required to keep.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your data. We share it only with service providers who help us operate (for example email, accounting or scheduling tools), and with regulators or authorities where the law requires it. Where a manufacturer warranty is registered, we may pass the necessary installation details to the manufacturer.

Cookies and storage

This website uses essential browser storage to remember your cookie choice. No analytics or advertising cookies are set unless you accept them. You can change your choice at any time by clearing this site’s storage in your browser.

Your rights

You have the right to access the personal data we hold about you, to ask us to correct or delete it, to restrict or object to processing, and to data portability. To exercise any of these, contact justimagineheating@gmail.com.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last reviewed.
