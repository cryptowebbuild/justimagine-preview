Landlord certificates

One less thing to chase.

If you let a property with gas appliances, you need a Gas Safety Record — often called a CP12 — every twelve months. It is not optional, and letting agents cannot sign it off for you.

We check every gas appliance and the pipework, issue the record and send you a digital copy the same day so you can forward it to tenants or your agent immediately.

We also note the renewal date and remind you before it is due, which is usually the part landlords appreciate most.

What we check

  • Gas appliances checked for safe operation and correct ventilation
  • Flue and chimney integrity checked for products of combustion
  • Gas pipework inspected and tested for soundness
  • Appliance safety devices and controls tested
  • Gas rates and burner pressures checked where accessible
  • A record issued for each appliance, with any defects clearly noted

Any appliance that fails is clearly recorded, with the reason and what needs to happen next.

The legal bit

What landlords are actually responsible for.

Legal duty

Landlords must have a gas safety check carried out every 12 months by a Gas Safe registered engineer.

Records

A copy of the Gas Safety Record must be given to existing tenants within 28 days and to new tenants before they move in.

Every property

The duty covers rented homes, flats, HMOs and any property with gas appliances — including holiday lets.

This is general guidance, not legal advice. If you are unsure about your obligations, check the current guidance from the Health and Safety Executive or speak to your letting agent.

Landlord FAQs

Paperwork, answered.

Need a certificate urgently? Call 07774 079 152.

How long does a gas safety check take?

Usually 30 to 60 minutes for a typical flat or house, depending on how many appliances there are and how accessible they are.

Can you deal with the tenant directly?

Yes. We can arrange access with the tenant and keep you copied in, which saves you acting as the middleman.

What happens if an appliance fails?

We record the defect on the record, explain the risk and isolate the appliance if it is unsafe. We can quote for the repair or replacement.

Do you cover HMOs and multiple properties?

Yes. We can book several properties in one visit window and keep the paperwork organised per address.

Is a CP12 the same as a service?

No. A gas safety check confirms the appliance is safe to use; a service also cleans and maintains it. Many landlords book both at the same time.
