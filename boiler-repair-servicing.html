Boiler repair

Fix the cause, not the symptom.

A boiler that keeps locking out is telling you something. Resetting it every morning might get you through the week, but it does not fix anything — and it often makes the eventual repair bigger.

We test the system properly, read the fault history and check the components around the boiler as well as the boiler itself. Then we explain what has failed, what it costs to put right and whether it is worth doing.

Most common repairs are straightforward and can be done on the first visit. If a part has to be ordered, we will tell you exactly what is happening and when we will be back.

Smell gas? Do not touch electrical switches. Open doors and windows, turn the gas off at the meter if safe, and call the National Gas Emergency Service on 0800 111 999 first.

Common faults

What we get called out for.

If yours is doing something not listed here, it is still worth a call.

No hot water

The boiler fires for heating but the taps stay cold — often a diverter valve, flow sensor or plate heat exchanger.

Lockout or fault code

The boiler stops and needs resetting. The code usually points somewhere specific; guessing is expensive.

Pressure keeps dropping

A sealed system should hold pressure. If it does not, something is leaking or the expansion vessel has failed.

Noisy or kettling

Rumbling, banging or whistling can mean limescale, sludge or a failing pump rather than a boiler on its way out.

Leaking water

Leaks from the boiler, a valve or pipework need finding before they damage ceilings and floors.

Radiators not heating

Cold at the bottom, cold at the top, or cold altogether — each points to a different cause.

Annual servicing

What a proper service actually includes.

A service is not a sticker and a quick look. It is a set of measurements and checks that catch problems while they are still cheap to fix.

Most manufacturers require an annual service to keep the warranty valid. We leave a written record so you have proof when you need it.

Book a service

Included in every service

  • Visual inspection of the boiler, flue and surrounding pipework
  • Flue gas analysis and combustion readings
  • Case seals, burner and heat exchanger checked and cleaned
  • Gas pressure and flow rate tested
  • Safety devices, expansion vessel and pressure checked
  • Condensate trap cleared and checked
  • Written service record for your warranty

Repair or replace?

An honest answer, not a sales pitch.

As a rough guide: if the boiler is under about eight years old, has had a good service history and the repair is a common part, repairing usually makes sense. If it is older, has had repeated failures, or the repair is close to the cost of a new unit, replacement is worth considering — especially once you factor in efficiency and warranty.

We will give you the numbers for both and let you decide. There is no pressure either way.

Repair FAQs

Before you book.

Still not sure? Call 07774 079 152 and describe the noise. We are good with noises.

Can you come out the same day?

We try to keep slots for emergencies and will always tell you honestly what is available. Call early in the day where you can.

Do you carry parts?

We carry common spares, but not every part for every boiler. If we need to order something we will tell you the lead time before you commit.

Is it worth servicing an old boiler?

If it is working reliably, yes — a service is cheaper than a breakdown and keeps you safe. If it is failing repeatedly, we will say so.

Can you repair a boiler under warranty?

We can attend, but work on a boiler still under manufacturer warranty may need to go through the manufacturer to keep the warranty intact. We will advise you before doing anything.
