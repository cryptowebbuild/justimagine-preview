Frequently asked questions.

Straight answers about gas safety, prices, coverage and how we work. If yours is not here, call us on 07774 079 152.

Gas Safe Reg. 6100115Rugby & Warwickshire
Are you Gas Safe registered?

Yes. Just Imagine Heating Ltd is on the Gas Safe Register under registration number 6100115. You can check it yourself on the Gas Safe Register website before we arrive — we would rather you did.

Which areas do you cover?

We are based in Rugby and cover Coventry, Leicester, Northampton, Leamington Spa, Warwick, Kenilworth, Milton Keynes and the towns and villages across Warwickshire. If you are just outside that list, call and ask.

How quickly can you come out?

For emergencies we aim to offer a same-day or next-day appointment where we can, and we will always give you a realistic time on the phone rather than an optimistic one.

Do you charge for a quote?

Quotes for planned work such as a new boiler or a radiator change are free. Where we need to diagnose a fault first, we will confirm any diagnostic charge with you before we visit.

Will I know the price before work starts?

Always. We explain what needs doing, what it will cost and what the options are before we pick up a tool. If we find something unexpected while working, we stop and talk to you before carrying on.

Do you work on all boiler brands?

We repair and service all major domestic boiler brands, including combi, system and heat-only boilers. If a part is unusual we will tell you straight away rather than ordering something on a guess.

Can you provide a landlord gas safety certificate?

Yes. We carry out landlord gas safety checks and issue CP12 Gas Safety Records for landlords and letting agents, with a digital copy sent the same day.

What should I do if I smell gas?

Do not turn lights or electrical switches on or off. Open doors and windows, turn the gas off at the meter if you can reach it safely, and call the National Gas Emergency Service on 0800 111 999 straight away. Once you are safe, call us and we will help with the repair.

Do you remind me when my service or certificate is due?

Yes. We note when your boiler service and gas safety certificate are due and get in touch before the date, so it does not become a last-minute panic.

How do I book?

Call 07774 079 152 or send a message through the quote form on this site. Tell us what is happening and where you are, and we will come back to you with the next available slot.
