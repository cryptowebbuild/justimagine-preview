Who we are

Good heating work should be boring.

It should turn up on time, be done tidily, cost what you were told it would cost, and still be working years later. That is the whole idea behind Just Imagine Heating.

We are a local, Gas Safe registered heating and plumbing company based in Rugby. That means the person who answers the phone understands the job, the person who quotes it is the person doing it, and there is no call centre in between.

We work on boilers, central heating, gas appliances and plumbing across Rugby, Coventry, Leicester, Northampton, Leamington Spa, Warwick, Kenilworth, Milton Keynes and the towns and villages in between.
